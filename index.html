What this means for your business

None of the projects above were built to impress. They were built because something was slow, scattered, or relied on one person remembering to do it. That's usually exactly what's going on inside a small business too: a manual process, a spreadsheet only one person understands, or a customer question that gets answered from scratch every time.

I don't start with "let's use AI." I start with the actual bottleneck, then build the smallest thing that fixes it, in a way that someone on your team who isn't technical can still run without me.

Seven years selling and supporting software into defence, pharmaceutical, aerospace and calibration lab customers also means I don't treat data protection as an afterthought, which matters the moment any tool touches customer information.
