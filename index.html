What this means for your business

The projects further down this page weren't built to impress. They were built because something was slow, scattered, or relied on one person remembering to do it. That's usually exactly what's going on inside a small business too: a manual process, a spreadsheet only one person understands, or a customer question that gets answered from scratch every time.

I don't start with "let's use AI." I start with the actual bottleneck, then build the smallest thing that fixes it, in a way that someone on your team who isn't technical can still run without me.

That background in regulated industries also means I don't treat data protection as an afterthought, which matters the moment any tool touches customer information.

Common questions

Is my data safe if I share it with you?

It's factored into how a project is scoped from the outset, not bolted on afterwards. If a build touches customer information, that shapes the approach from day one, not something addressed once it's already built.

What if nobody on my team is technical?

That's normal, and expected. Everything I build is designed to be run by whoever's already doing the job, not by someone who understands how it works underneath.

What does it cost?

That depends entirely on the problem, which is exactly why there's no fixed number on this page. What is fixed is the process: a first build with an agreed scope and fee, decided before anything starts.

What if I don't know what to ask for?

That's usually the hardest part, and also the part I can help with. The first conversation is often what surfaces the actual bottleneck.
